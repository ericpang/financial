Financial planning services in Singapore

One planner looks after the whole picture, so your retirement, investments and insurance all pull in the same direction.

  • Retirement planning

    Work out the monthly income you will need and plan around CPF LIFE, SRS and your own savings to get there.

  • Investment advisory

    A diversified, low-cost portfolio matched to how much risk you can live with, reviewed every year.

  • Insurance planning

    Check you are covered for what matters, without paying for overlapping policies you don't need.

  • Estate planning

    Wills, CPF nominations and lasting power of attorney, so the people you love are looked after.

  • Tax planning

    Use reliefs such as SRS top-ups and CPF cash top-ups to keep more of what you earn, legally and simply.

How it works

No jargon, no hard sell. Here is what happens after you book.

  1. Have a free chat

    A 45-minute call or coffee about your goals, savings, CPF and worries. You leave with three priority actions either way.

  2. Get your plan

    Within two weeks you receive a written plan in plain English, with the fee agreed before any work starts.

  3. Grow it together

    We check in twice a year and whenever life changes: a new job, a baby, a home or retirement.

Questions people ask before booking

Straight answers about cost, CPF and what to expect.

How much does the first consultation cost?

Nothing. The first 45-minute consultation is free, with no obligation to buy any product.

Do I need a lot of money to start financial planning?

No. Many clients start with a monthly budget and an emergency fund. A plan is most useful early, when small habits have the longest time to grow.

What happens in the first meeting?

We talk about your goals, current savings, CPF, insurance and timeline. You leave with three priority actions, whether or not you work with us.

How does Horizon Wealth get paid?

We charge a flat planning fee agreed in writing before any work starts, and we disclose any product commission up front.

Can you help with CPF and retirement in Singapore?

Yes. We plan around CPF LIFE, SRS contributions and your cash savings to estimate a realistic monthly retirement income.

Is my personal information safe?

We only ask for what we need to contact you, never ask for passwords or bank logins, and handle personal data in line with Singapore's PDPA.
